restitutor

Paste a general-relativity display equation; the engine restores or strips its constants. Lookup, never inference — anything it cannot vouch for, it declines with the offending symbols named.

Stage-1 paste box over the production engine (src/unitsEngine.ts, GR registry). The generator-parameterized convention layer is src/convention.ts; the domain spec is docs/unit-systems-census.md.
